01 · The problem

You shoot the wedding.
The follow-up shoots you.

You booked twelve weddings this season. Each one is an advance, a shoot-day balance, an album deadline and a client who'll ask "photos kab tak?" — and it all lives in a chat thread, a notes app, and your memory. All three quietly leak.

💸

The final balance is easy to forget

Advance came in months ago. The album-delivery balance is the one you actually forget to collect — you hand over the album and the last payment just... drifts.

📅

Album deadlines slip silently

"Four to six weeks" turns into three months. There's no single list telling you which albums are due this week and which have already gone overdue.

🧵

Every shoot is a separate WhatsApp thread

Ten live weddings, ten scattered chats. Reconstructing who's paid what and what you still owe them means scrolling through months of messages.

📸

You're an artist, not an accounts clerk

The part you love is the shoot and the edit. Chasing balances and deadlines is the part that eats your evenings and dents client goodwill.

02 · The insight

A shoot isn't one payment on one day — it's a timeline: advance, shoot-day, album balance, each with its own deadline — yet it's tracked in your head.

StudioBook's whole job is to make that timeline the app: book the shoot, split it into milestones and deliverables with dates, and let it chase the balances and album deadlines for you — so the money and the album both land, without you being the reminder.

07 · Who it's for & questions

Built for the studio that shoots, not the enterprise that files.

StudioBook is for wedding & event photographers and videographers — a solo shooter or a small team running a dozen live weddings. Big enough that tracking advances and album deadlines eats your week; small enough that a full CRM suite is overkill. If you're running it on WhatsApp and memory, this is for you.

Wedding photographers Videographers Event & corporate shoots Small studios & solos
Does StudioBook actually send the WhatsApp reminders?

StudioBook drafts each reminder — personalised, with the client's name, the pending balance or the album deadline — and stages it in your outbox. You send it from your own number with a tap, so it always reads like it came from you, not a robot.

How do payment milestones work?

Every booking's package is split into milestones — typically a booking advance, a shoot-day payment and an album-delivery balance — each with its own due date and paid amount. Record a payment (UPI or cash) and the milestone clears; the outstanding balance updates instantly across the dashboard.

Can it track album and reel delivery deadlines?

Yes. Each booking carries its deliverables — edited photos, the album, reels — with a delivery deadline. The dashboard surfaces what's due soon and flags any album that has gone overdue, so a delivery never quietly slips past its date.

I run a small studio. Is this overkill?

It's built for exactly that — a solo photographer or a small team juggling a dozen live weddings. Add each booking once and StudioBook keeps the whole pipeline: who's paid what, which album is due, and who needs a nudge today.

What does it cost?

Pricing is worked out on a quick discovery call and billed monthly on subscription. We're keeping public prices off the page while we onboard our first studios.
